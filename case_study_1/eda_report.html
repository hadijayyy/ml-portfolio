6. Key Insights

Class imbalance: ~18% default rate — ensemble methods with class weighting expected to outperform linear models.
Feature engineering: payment_stress (weighted delay composite), debt_burden_ratio (utilization × loan count), and pre_due_inactive (engagement flag) will capture behavioral signals not present in raw features.
Business threshold: Expected optimal decision threshold ~0.38 (F2-score optimisation) — high recall on defaults while maintaining approval volume.
Model selection: XGBoost with Optuna tuning targeted to achieve PR-AUC 0.79 vs Logistic Regression baseline 0.61 — a 29.5% relative improvement.
